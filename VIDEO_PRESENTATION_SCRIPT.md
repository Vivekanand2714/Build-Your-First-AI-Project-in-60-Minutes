## 🎬 Video Recording Checklist
* **Screen Resolution:** 1920 × 1080 (16:9 full HD)
* **Local Server:** Ensure `http://localhost:5173/` is running
* **Recording Tool:** Windows Game Bar (`Win + Alt + R`), OBS Studio, or Loom
* **Audio:** Clear microphone, quiet background, no distracting background music
* **Presentation Mode:** You can also run the interactive presentation studio directly at `http://localhost:5173/presentation`

### Chapter 2: The Growth Strategy (0:25 – 0:50)
* **Visual on Screen:**  
  Clean diagram showing the cyclical viral loop:  
  $$\text{Discover} \longrightarrow \text{Register} \longrightarrow \text{Get Referral Link} \longrightarrow \text{Share With Classmates} \longrightarrow \text{Campus Challenge} \longrightarrow \text{More Registrations} \circlearrowleft$$  
  Subtle emerald indicator highlighting each step as spoken.

* **Voiceover:**  
  > *"Instead of treating this as just a workshop registration campaign, I designed the product itself to support acquisition and referrals. My core growth loop is simple: a student discovers the workshop, registers, gets a referral link, shares it with classmates, and those registrations contribute to their campus leaderboard. On a ₹2,000 budget, peer referrals in college WhatsApp groups are our primary acquisition engine."*
